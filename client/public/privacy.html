Basionai 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 22 日 · 开发者：basion wang · 联系邮箱：basionwang@gmail.com

Basionai（以下简称"本应用"）是你家中电脑上 AI 助手（HermesPet）的手机遥控端。我们高度重视你的隐私，本政策说明我们收集哪些信息、如何使用，以及你拥有的权利。

一、我们收集的信息

1. 账号信息

当你注册或登录时，我们会收集你的 电子邮箱地址 和密码，用于创建账号、登录验证，以及把你的手机与你的电脑关联起来（手机与电脑登录同一账号即可在云端自动配对）。密码以加密形式保存。

2. 你发出的指令与对话

本应用的核心功能是"在手机上给你自己的电脑派活"。你输入或语音转写的指令文字，会通过我们的中继服务器（relay.hermespet.cc）转发到与你账号关联的那台电脑，或在同一局域网下直接发送。中继服务器仅用于把指令送达你的设备，我们不会将你的对话内容用于广告，也不会出售给第三方。对话历史主要保存在你自己的手机与电脑本地。

3. 语音输入

当你使用语音派活时，本应用会请求麦克风权限，并使用设备的语音识别能力把语音转成文字。语音识别可能由 Apple 的系统服务处理，受 Apple 相应条款约束。

4. 照片与文件

仅当你主动选择上传图片或文件作为附件时，本应用才会在你授权下访问对应内容，用于发送给你的电脑处理。

二、设备权限

  • 麦克风：语音派活
  • 语音识别：把语音转成文字指令
  • 照片 / 文件：仅在你选择附件时访问
  • 本地网络：在同一 WiFi 下与你的电脑直连

三、我们不会做的事

本应用不包含任何第三方广告或追踪 SDK，不会出售你的个人信息，不会将你的对话内容用于与提供服务无关的用途。

四、数据的保存与删除

你可以随时在 设置 → 删除账号 中注销账号；删除后，我们会清除你的账号及与之关联的云端数据，此操作不可撤销。你也可以在 设置 → 退出登录 中清除本机登录信息。

五、演示模式

未登录时点击"先逛逛 · 体验演示"进入演示模式，期间仅展示内置的示例数据，不收集任何个人信息。

六、儿童

本应用不面向 14 周岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策，更新后会修改顶部的"生效日期"。重大变更我们会在应用内或官网告知。

八、联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：basionwang@gmail.com

Basionai Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026 · Developer: basion wang · Contact: basionwang@gmail.com

Basionai ("the App") is a mobile remote control for the AI assistant (HermesPet) running on your home computer. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1. Account information

When you register or sign in, we collect your email address and password to create your account, authenticate you, and pair your phone with your computer (signing in to the same account on both links them automatically). Passwords are stored in encrypted form.

2. Commands and conversations you send

The App's core function is to send tasks from your phone to your own computer. The text you type or dictate is forwarded through our relay server (relay.hermespet.cc) to the computer linked to your account, or sent directly over your local network. The relay is used solely to deliver commands to your devices; we do not use your conversation content for advertising and do not sell it to third parties. Conversation history is stored primarily on your own phone and computer.

3. Voice input

When you use voice, the App requests microphone access and uses on-device speech recognition to convert speech to text. Speech recognition may be processed by Apple's system services, subject to Apple's terms.

4. Photos and files

Only when you choose to attach a photo or file does the App access that content, with your permission, to send it to your computer.

2. Device Permissions

  • Microphone: voice commands
  • Speech Recognition: converting speech to text
  • Photos / Files: only when you pick an attachment
  • Local Network: direct connection to your computer on the same Wi‑Fi

3. What We Do Not Do

The App contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, does not sell your personal information, and does not use your conversation content for any purpose unrelated to providing the service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete your account at any time via Settings → Delete Account; this removes your account and associated cloud data and cannot be undone. You may also clear local login data via Settings → Sign Out.

5. Demo Mode

Tapping "Explore the demo" before signing in enters a demo mode that shows only built-in sample data and collects no personal information.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the "Effective date" above. We will notify you of material changes in the App or on our website.

8. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: basionwang@gmail.com
